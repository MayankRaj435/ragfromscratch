
COMPANY LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave should be requested through the employee portal and approved by the employee's manager.

Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. If an employee is absent for more than two consecutive working days, supporting medical documentation may be required.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least five working days before the intended leave date. Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward for up to five days, subject to company policy. Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the following calendar year.

For questions about leave balances or approvals, employees should contact the Human Resources department.
